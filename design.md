## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of competitive academic excellence, grounded guidance, and forward-looking achievement rooted in the motto "We Create Future". The brand serves students, ambitious aspirants, parents, and educators across competitive, secondary, and professional educational streams. 

The aesthetic is **Corporate / Modern infused with Energetic Academic Clarity**:
- **Foundation & Authority:** Anchor layouts with deep royal navy tones that signal trust, discipline, and pedigree.
- **Inspirational Vibrancy:** Leverage warm radiant orange and vivid crimson to celebrate milestone achievements, notifications, scholarship callouts, and performance stats.
- **Growth & Vitality:** Utilize crisp emerald green accents to symbolize continuous progress, batch enrollments, test accuracy, and success metrics.
- **Tone & Persona:** Inspiring yet disciplined, welcoming yet deeply professional, structured for high-focus reading and low cognitive strain during intense test-prep scenarios.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the identity iconography:

- **Primary (`#20337A`):** The core institutional Royal Navy Blue. Used for top application navigation, primary action buttons, key section headlines, and major structural surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#F26A21`):** Warm Radiant Orange, inspired by the sacred Tilak and the energetic lower globe hemisphere. Applied to primary call-to-actions (e.g., "Enroll Now", "Take Test"), pending status indicators, and motivational badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D32F2F`):** Vivid Crimson Red. Reserved for high-urgency notifications, test countdown timers, rank drops, live exam alerts, and selective typographical emphasis.
- **Accent Emerald (`#138844`):** Supporting growth green taken from the ascending laurel leaves. Used for verified status badges, passing grades, fee confirmations, and upward trajectory indicators.
- **Neutral Core (`#111827`):** Midnight Slate used for body text, guaranteeing AAA accessibility ratios against clean white canvas layers.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:** Main background is pure white (`#FFFFFF`), supported by subtle tinted canvas layers (`#F8FAFC` and soft cool slate `#F0F4FA`) for grouped dashboard widgets, card containers, and side rails.

## Typography

The typographic system relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, bridging geometric modernity with welcoming humanist touches.

- **Headlines & Display:** Bold to Extra-Bold weights (700/800) with slight negative tracking provide an authoritative academic identity reminiscent of textbook titles and official notices.
- **Body & Instructional Copy:** Regular weight (400) set at 14px to 16px with relaxed 1.5–1.6x line heights to sustain eye comfort over lengthy test series descriptions, answer explanations, and syllabus breakdowns.
- **Labels & Tags:** Set in Medium to Bold (600/700) with subtle uppercase tracking for course codes, batch badges (e.g., "JEE ADVANCED", "FOUNDATION 2026"), and scorecard data tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system follows an accessible 8px modular baseline grid, prioritizing structured dashboard portals, mock-test workflows, and informational course landing pages:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1200px to 1440px max-width container, 24px gutters, and 32px outer canvas margins. Split view layouts are common (e.g., Question Sheet 8 cols, Question Navigator 4 cols).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 16px gutters, 24px margins. Panels collapse into vertical stacks with persistent bottom or tab navigation.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 12px gutters, 16px margins. Dense data cards convert into single-column vertical cards with thumb-accessible sticky triggers.
- **Rhythm & Density:** Academic tables and mock test interfaces use compact spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`), while promotional course showcases and institutional marketing pages utilize expansive spacing (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **tinted tonal layering combined with subtle, crisp ambient shadows**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` base.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Modules):** White cards sitting on `#F8FAFC` or `#F0F4FA` backgrounds, elevated by a soft navy-tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(32, 51, 122, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Hover States):** Lifted cards on hover and dropdowns utilize `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(32, 51, 122, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Sticky Bars):** High-priority alerts, mock test timers, and dialogs use `0 20px 32px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.18)` accompanied by a semi-opaque navy backdrop blur (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).
- **Outlines:** Low-contrast borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) keep test sheets and question grids cleanly defined without visual clutter.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Balanced Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Selectors, Regular Buttons):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius. Delivers a clean, friendly yet institutional look.
- **Large Containers (Course Cards, Test Question Containers, Testimonial Blocks):** `1rem` (16px) radius for clear visual containment.
- **Pills & Badges:** Full circular/pill radius (`9999px`) for status chips, batch badges, and question pallete indicators (e.g., "Answered", "Marked for Review").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Royal Navy Blue (`#20337A`) background, white text, 8px border radius, medium shadow. Hover state brightens toward Royal Blue (`#29388B`).
- **Urgent Action / Admission CTA:** Radiant Orange (`#F26A21`) background with bold white typography; on hover, shifts to darker vermilion.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#20337A`, navy text; fills with `#F0F4FA` on hover.

### Question Palette & State Chips
- **Answered / Completed:** Emerald Green (`#138844`) solid fill or border with green badge.
- **Marked for Review:** Warm Radiant Orange (`#F26A21`) solid or ring.
- **Unattempted / Critical Notice:** Crimson Red (`#D32F2F`) text with subtle soft-pink tint background (`#FEF2F2`).
- **Category / Stream Chips:** Subtle navy tint background (`#F0F4FA`), `#20337A` bold label, pill-shaped.

### Cards & Modules
- **Course & Batch Card:** White surface, 16px radius, subtle border (`#E2E8F0`), with a 4px primary-navy or orange top accent strip to denote the department or subject stream.
- **Result & Performance Card:** Displays student rank and percentile with large display typography, accented with emerald growth arrows and subtle progress radial bars.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Input Fields:** 8px radius, `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`. On focus: 2px border in `#20337A` with a soft outer ring glow (`rgba(32, 51, 122, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Colored in `#20337A` when selected, with crisp white checks and dot glyphs.

### Academic Notice Banners
- Top-anchored notice bar in deep navy (`#20337A`) with red (`#D32F2F`) "NEW" badge and animated marquee or link pills for immediate student updates.
