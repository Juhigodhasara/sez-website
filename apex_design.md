## Brand & Style

The design system embodies academic rigor, prestigious mentorship, and ambitious upward mobility tailored for high-stakes test preparation (IIT-JEE, NEET, Civil Services). It combines institutional credibility with high-energy modern human learning. The visual tone is commanding yet approachable, projecting unwavering authority, intellectual clarity, and authentic warmth.

The target audience encompasses determined aspirants seeking top-tier ranks, alongside discerning parents evaluating pedagogical credibility, faculty reputation, and historical outcomes. The emotional response must evoke unshakable confidence, focused motivation, clarity under pressure, and institutional trust.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate/Modern** precision with **Tactile Cleanliness**:
- Crisp, mathematically balanced modular layouts that minimize cognitive overhead.
- High-contrast visual anchoring using deep maritime blues layered over pristine paper-like surfaces.
- Strategic bursts of energetic golden-amber accents to signify breakthroughs, rank milestones, key call-to-actions, and live mentorship badges.
- Sophisticated micro-interactions: smooth spring-based toggles, subtle directional hover states, and tactile card feedback designed to drive enrollment conversions.

## Colors

The system employs a purposeful four-tier color scheme engineered to convey institutional heritage, technological precision, and academic vitality:

- **Primary (`#0D2137`) — Deep Navy Slate:** Anchors primary structural frames, master navigation bars, high-prominence display typography, and dominant institutional surfaces. Establishes indisputable gravitas, stability, and focus.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`) — Vibrant Cobalt Blue:** Serves as the interactive workhorse. Applied to primary navigational actions, batch enrollment links, interactive tabs, active filters, and progress bars.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`) — Warm Golden Amber:** Represents academic excellence, rank achievements, scholarship alerts, and high-urgency conversion touchpoints (e.g., "Scholarship Test Open", "Limited Seats Left"). Used deliberately for badges, stat highlights, and critical conversion catalysts without creating visual noise.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`) — Slate Gray:** Governs supporting copy, inactive states, tabular borders, and structural metadata.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF` for core reading and examination interfaces to maximize contrast.
- **Surface Soft Tint:** `#F8FAFC` for high-density dashboard layouts and alternating module panels.
- **Surface Section Accent:** `#F1F5F9` for structured grouping, course syllabus cards, and testimonial blocks.
- **Border Subtle:** `#E2E8F0` for hairline dividers (1px) creating soft containment.
- **Border Focus/Active:** `#93C5FD` for focused form states and selected batch cards.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an authoritative academic environment while maintaining reading endurance during prolonged study sessions. 

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Chosen for its geometric confidence, humanist terminals, and contemporary authority. Tighter negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) lends headlines a modern editorial weight that commands trust.
- **Body & Long-form Content (Inter):** Deployed across all descriptive text, test question modules, fee tables, and analytics. Tall x-height and open apertures guarantee maximum legibility under rapid skimming or high screen resolution variations.
- **Micro-copy & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Semi-bold and bold weights with open tracking (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) ensure distinct structural categorization for rank badges, batch status, countdown timers, and button callouts.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid governs desktop and tablet breakpoints, transitioning to a flexible 4-column system on mobile devices.

### Form Factors & Breakpoints
- **Desktop Large (≥1440px):** Max container width `1280px` centered with auto-margins; 12 columns, `gutter: 2rem`, section margins `5rem`.
- **Desktop (1024px – 1439px):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer canvas margins `3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, canvas margins `2rem`. Academic comparison tables horizontally scroll with sticky left headers.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, canvas margins `1.25rem`. Two-column card rows collapse into single stacks or horizontal snap carousels for batch profiles and faculty rosters.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Component Interiors:** Utilize `space-sm` (8px) for tightly grouped chip sets, `space-md` (16px) for inner form element spacing, and `space-lg` (24px) for robust card padding.
- **Section Stack Rhythm:** Vertical distance between instructional blocks adheres strictly to `space-xl` (40px) or `space-xl * 2` (80px) on desktop to preserve academic calm and prevent visual congestion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined combination of **tonal surface stacking** and **soft ambient shadows tinted with Deep Navy**. Pure black drop shadows are prohibited.

### Tonal Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Floor):** Pure Canvas (`#FFFFFF`) or Structural Canvas (`#F8FAFC`). Flat, un-elevated.
2. **Level 1 (Subtle Rest):** Elevated cards, module directories, and study material indices (`#FFFFFF` background paired with a `1px` border of `#E2E8F0` and `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(13, 33, 55, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(13, 33, 55, 0.04)`).
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Focal State):** Active batch cards, selected test series, and interactive calculator cards (`box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -3px rgba(13, 33, 55, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(13, 33, 55, 0.04)`). Card scales slightly (`transform: translateY(-2px)`).
4. **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Admission inquiry modals, rank predictor drawers, and sticky conversion bars (`box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -10px rgba(13, 33, 55, 0.14), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(13, 33, 55, 0.06)`).

### Glass Tinting
Navigation bars utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `#FFFFFF` with `90%` opacity and an underlying border-bottom in `#E2E8F0`, keeping header presence light without losing contrast during high-speed vertical scrolls.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature to balance academic discipline with modern digital friendliness:

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Standard inputs, small trigger badges, secondary buttons, tooltips, and exam question option selectors.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Course cards, faculty spotlights, pricing containers, and student testimonial cards.
- **Heroic Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Major hero breakout modules, scholarship application banners, and parent advisory consultation blocks.
- **Capsule Form (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Live indicator badges, category filter pills, rank badges (e.g., "AIR 1"), and high-conversion floating WhatsApp/Direct Counseling action anchors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Cobalt):** Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `none`, font `label-lg`, radius `0.5rem` (8px). Height `48px` on desktop, horizontal padding `24px`. Hover state: `#1D4ED8` with subtle Level 1 tinted elevation. Active: `#1E40AF`.
- **Conversion Accent (Amber Golden):** Background `#F59E0B`, text `#0D2137`, font weight `700`. Used exclusively for high-intent actions ("Book Free Demo", "Apply for Scholarship"). Hover: `#D97706`.
- **Secondary Outline:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #0D2137`, text `#0D2137`. Hover: background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Background transparent, text `#2563EB`. Hover: background `#EFF6FF`.

### Cards & Batch Containers
- Base background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `16px` to `24px` (`rounded-lg` or `rounded-xl`), framed with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Card structure must contain:
  1. Top metadata bar (Target exam year, batch mode: Offline/Hybrid).
  2. Faculty credentials with small circular avatars.
  3. Feature checklist with checkmark icons in `#2563EB`.
  4. Bottom-anchored action button with a pricing/seat-capacity status strip.

### Chips & Rank Badges
- **Status/Live Chips:** Height `28px`, capsule radius (`9999px`), padding `4px 12px`. For live classes: background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E` with a pulsating amber dot indicator (`#F59E0B`).
- **Filter Chips:** Height `36px`, rounded `8px`. Inactive: background `#F1F5F9`, text `#64748B`. Active: background `#0D2137`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `8px`, padding `0 16px`, font `body-lg`.
- Focus state: border color `#2563EB`, outer ring `3px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`. No raw browser outlines.
- Error state: border color `#EF4444`, message in `body-sm` (`#DC2626`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px x 20px`, radius `4px` for checkboxes, `50%` for radio buttons.
- Default state: border `2px solid #94A3B8`.
- Checked state: background `#2563EB`, border color `#2563EB`, centered sharp icon in pure white.

### Lists & Curriculum Accordions
- Module listings use stacked accordions with `1px solid #E2E8F0` separation and `16px` inner padding.
- Left-side numeric index with fixed width (`32px`) styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold (`#64748B`). Expanded state transitions to `#F8FAFC` background with a `2px solid #2563EB` left-accent indicator.

### Dedicated Academic Components
