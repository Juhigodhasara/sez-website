Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents and students need to know regarding admissions, batch sizes, and methodology.

We provide structured academic coaching for Class 1 through Class 12 across CBSE, ICSE, and State Boards. For Classes 11 and 12, we offer specialized faculty cohorts in Science (PCM & PCB), Commerce, and Humanities.
We adhere to a strict policy: a maximum of 12 to 15 students per batch. This allows our mentors to address individual learning difficulties and ensure every student actively participates during discussions.
In addition to classroom time, we host daily 1-on-1 Doubt Windows from 6:30 PM to 8:00 PM. Shy students can book individual 15-minute slots with their mentors to ask questions in private.
Yes, absolutely. We encourage every new student to attend 2 complimentary demo sessions in their respective stream. There are zero upfront fees or long-term lock-in obligations.
